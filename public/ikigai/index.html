What makes your days worth getting up for?

Ikigai is an everyday Japanese word. It doesn't mean a grand destiny or a job title — in Japan it's used about ordinary things. A garden. A grandchild. The walk you take each morning. Most people's ikigai is made of small, repeated things.

This is four questions about your own life. There's no score and no right answer. At the end you'll get your four circles drawn, and a few sentences built from what you actually said — not a personality type.
